## Shortcut

**Ports wake every waiter, not one (slice W, PW not built).** A port with several threads blocked in `port_wait` wakes all of them when a binding becomes ready; the losers poll, find nothing and sleep again (correct, but a thundering herd), and `port_wait` still scans every binding on each poll (O(bindings), fine for the tens a server has, poor for thousands).

## Why it was acceptable

Correctness and the targeted wake are done; one waiter per port is the common case.

## Proper fix, and when

The exact ready set with a first-come list and the wake-one baton of W-SPEC section 3 (PW). **Once the baton exists, add a thread pool per port to the service runtime** (slice R's one-thread-per-port rule holds only until then). Also found by the security review: a sleeping port waiter makes *every* syscall and trap on the system re-evaluate that port (145 us at 6,144 bindings, 1.5 ms at 65,536, under the big lock), so evaluate a port only when a bound object was touched, and keep `queued_bytes` as a counter. Plan: resolved by SCHED-SMP-SPEC task 21.
