## Shortcut

On 2026-11-18, PR 243's `host` job failed in `OS crates under the loom and shuttle model checkers`: `klock`'s `the_lock_excludes` (shuttle, randomised schedules) panicked with "exceeded max_steps bound 1000000. this might be caused by an unfair schedule (e.g., a spin loop)" on one random schedule (`klock` was not touched by the PR). A re-run is expected to pass. Shuttle's random scheduler can starve a thread that spins on the lock for a million steps.

## Why it was acceptable

It is intermittent, and the model has caught real problems (its negative control, `without_the_lock_the_model_loses_updates`, is the proof that it checks).

## Risk

A red host job for no reason costs a re-run and trains the habit of ignoring red.

## Proper fix, and when

Give the spin loop in the model a yield point shuttle can schedule around (`shuttle::thread::yield_now()` in the model's spin), or raise `max_steps` for this test, or use shuttle's PCT scheduler for it; then run the test a few thousand times to show it does not fail. Soon (it touches every PR's host job).
