## Shortcut

**Kernel stack slots are never returned** (slice T step 1): a slot keeps its four frames and its mapping once made and goes on a free list, so memory held is the peak thread count times 16 KiB, and `spawn` panics (`expect`) if no slot or frame can be made, as heap exhaustion did before. A remote hart's stale kernel translation could otherwise point a reused slot at the wrong frame.

## Why it was acceptable

Bounded by the global thread cap; frames are plentiful (256 MiB).

## Proper fix, and when

A shootdown on shrink (the infrastructure exists), a fallible `spawn` that returns NO_MEMORY, and per-job charging of stacks (slice T steps 4 and 5).
