**What.** In the epic's smoke (run 45340938910, `kernel (smoke a)`, 2026-11-24) `cpu_time_is_counted_per_thread_and_process` failed with "a thread that slept 50 ms was charged 7595800 ns"; every other test of the boot passed. A hosted runner's wall-clock timing under load, like T178 and T219; not reproduced locally.
**Why acceptable.** One occurrence, a timing bound on a shared runner; the accounting itself is covered by the host tests.
**Risk.** A flaky gate.
**Proper fix.** Read the test's bound (it compares CPU time with a sleep): make it relative to what the same thread was charged awake, or count in the icount boots, where time is deterministic; or move it to the `-icount` shard.
