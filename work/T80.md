## Shortcut

**Only the launcher and the tests set job priority limits** (task 8): a job made by any other creator (`init`'s session and driver jobs, the shell's own children, the suite) inherits the root's `max_prio` 63 and RT budget, so those programs may raise their threads into the real-time band. The launcher gives every program job 20 and no budget (SCHED-SMP-SPEC 4.9, Q4). RT budgets are accounted but not enforced until task 25, so a real-time spinner in an unrestricted job can starve the system.

## Why it was acceptable

Only trusted boot code creates the unrestricted jobs.

## Proper fix, and when

Policy for `init`'s session job (31) and devmgr's driver jobs; enforcement (task 25); a default of the normal band for jobs made by a process that is itself confined.
