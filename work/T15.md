## Shortcut

**Service death policy = rebuild the whole stack** (S4): `init` still rebuilds `memfsd`, `launcher`, `consoled` and `sh` together and `memfsd` state is lost on restart. (The registry service exists since slice SD, and the network stack publishes through it; the old servers and the clients' reconnect-on-`UNAVAILABLE` are SD step 4, not done.)

## Why it was acceptable

Memory filesystem has nothing to keep; keeps S4 small.

## Proper fix, and when

A name/registry service with reconnect, and real state recovery with the disk filesystem (S6+).
