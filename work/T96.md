## Shortcut

**`netcore` TCP (S9 step 4a) leftovers**:

- (1) the orphan pool's 512 KiB byte bound is not enforced (only the 32-connection and 20 s bounds; the bytes are charged to the account while it lives and are small per connection)
- (2) `tcp_set_nodelay` is a no-op (NODELAY is fixed at connect time in `TcpConfig`)
- (3) `Core::next_deadline` does not say that output is pending: the adapter must call `egress` after every ingress and client call, which `netd`'s loop does
- (4) a connection that reaches TIME-WAIT is replaced by a record at once and its pending final ACK is sent from the conversion, not from the connection
- (5) RST and challenge-ACK limiters are per core, as the spec says
- (6) [resolved by S9 step 5: `netsup` takes 32 bytes from `/svc/random` and gives `netd` a derived key; zeros remain only for host tests that inject their own].

## Why it was acceptable

Host-only and covered by `netcore/tests/tcp.rs` (12 scenarios and a 15-seed hostile-link run).

## Risk

(1) a program that leaves many large connections draining can hold more than 512 KiB of the stack's memory for 20 s, bounded by its own account.

## Proper fix, and when

Step 4b (accounts and orphans in `netd`), step 5 (the secret).
