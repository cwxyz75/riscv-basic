## Shortcut

**The crash harness is a single-device, single-thread model**:

- (1) the independent checker of FS-SPEC check 5 (a host `fsck` written from the spec by a separate context) does not exist; `check_fs` and `check` were written with the code (the `memfs` differential and the crash model are the independent parts)
- (2) crash points are between device operations: no reordering of flushed against unflushed writes beyond the drop/keep/tear patterns sampled (not every subset), and tearing is half a block, not per-sector
- (3) the three-operation workloads are sampled (60 by default, `DOOFS_CRASH3=n`), not exhaustive
- (4) no attribute operations in the set until step 6
- (5) the test volume is 120 blocks (the reserve is half of it)
- (6) the corruption test flips a stride of bits, not all (a full run is `DOOFS_*` future work).

## Why it was acceptable

The two-operation set is exhaustive over its operations; the model is an independent implementation.

## Proper fix, and when

(1) a separate-context review slice before 5f; (6) in the soak tier.
