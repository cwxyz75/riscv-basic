id: T240
kind: tidy
title: KT14: panic-path defects (console `LINE` is shared after `break_lock`; the panic trace may read through a guard page)
area: kernel
horizon: long
refs: [KT14, L20]
---

**What.** (1) `console.rs` `write_str` and `write_user` form `&mut LINE` on the claim that interrupts are off; the real guard is the UART lock, and `console::break_lock()` (the panic handler, `fatal`) zeroes `UART_OWNER` without stopping other harts, so a panicking hart can form a second `&mut LINE` while another is inside it (garbled line state). (2) `main.rs` panic `Trace` reads `[sp, top)` with `top` from `PerHart.kstack_top`, written only on a return to user mode, so on a kernel thread or a migrated thread `top` can be another slot's: the walk crosses a guard page and the page fault inside the panic handler hides the real panic.
**Why acceptable.** Both only while already panicking: they cost diagnostics.
**Proper fix.** (1) Keep the line state per hart or in the UART lock's guard. (2) Bound the walk by `KSTACK_BYTES` and require `sp` and `top` in one slot.
