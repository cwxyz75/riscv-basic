## Shortcut

**No start gate for pipeline stages; the Ctrl-C race is closed another way.** A stage runs at once and `sh` registers the job right after. A Ctrl-C before the acknowledgement reaches `consoled` with no job, but its 0x03 byte waits on the terminal and `sh` reads it after the acknowledgement and kills the stages itself (tested with `spin` and Ctrl-C in one write). What remains: a program can use the few milliseconds to do harm (nothing more than any program can); and the 0x03 is only seen if `consoled` could deliver it (not under an exhausted IPC budget, T2).

## Why it was acceptable

Simple, and no `rt` startup change.

## Proper fix, and when

The specified start channel, once programs need a gate for other reasons.
