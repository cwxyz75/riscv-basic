title: The fault-early-reap control proves a missing record panics, not that a reused stack is caught
---

## Shortcut

`fault-early-reap` (L11d, PR 264) reaps the exiting thread before its switch, record and stack together; the switch then finds no record and the kernel panics, which `teardown_with_threads_on_other_harts` reports as a crash. The Fable review (2026-11-19) pointed out a faithful control frees only the stack and keeps the record: that was tried, and nothing detects it, because a freed `KStack` is neither unmapped nor poisoned, so the thread simply goes on running on memory that is still intact.

## Why acceptable

The record-removal variant still shows the harness detects a premature reap; the real protection is the big lock (T163) until L12.

## Proper fix, and when

A debug feature of `KStack`: poison a stack on free (and fill the guard), so a thread still running on a freed stack, or a stack handed out again while in use, fails the guard check ("stack of thread ... was overwritten") the way SCHED-SMP-SPEC 3.6 expects. Then make `fault-early-reap` free only the stack. With L12.
