title: The shuttle model has no reacquire-then-reap-prev step (the reference walks now run inside the G4 window)

## Shortcut

L12a (PR 266) released the big lock at a context switch, but `kcore/tests/sched2_kernel_walk.rs` still has `bkl_barrier` (its hart holds the lock until the switch is finished, the old K3), so no wake, pull or reap by another hart runs inside a block or exit switch window there; and the shuttle G4 mode of `rq_concurrency` has no "reacquire, then reap `prev`" step (the model's reaper uses the model's own execution record, not the kernel's rule). Found by the Fable review (2026-11-19), which also found the `reap_prev` use-after-free the models could not see. The deterministic tests in `sched_transit_g4.rs` cover the rules one at a time.

## Why acceptable

The rules are covered pointwise, the k boots at 1, 2 and 4 pass and the high finding is fixed.

## Proper fix, and when

Drop `bkl_barrier` in the walk for the block, sleep and exit paths (interleave other harts' wake, pull and reap between the operation and `switched`), and give the shuttle model a `prev` per hart that is consumed after a variable delay with the kernel's `reap_if_dead` rule (detached, exited, not in transit). Do it before L12b.

## Note (2026-11-19, L12a)

The reference walk (`sched2_kernel_walk`) no longer has a barrier: other harts' operations run while a switch is open, and the old Runnable-only transit is caught there (`double dispatch`). Remaining: the shuttle model's `prev` step.
