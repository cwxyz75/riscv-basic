## Shortcut

**A flaky volume test in the fail-demo build** (2026-11-17): twice in two PRs the `harness (fail-demo)` job failed on a volume test (the volume manager test, then the symlink test after it) with status -7 (peer closed) on the first request to a freshly started volume server, and passed on rerun; three local runs of the same kernel were clean. Probable cause: `volmgr` published the names as soon as it started `fsd`, so a client could be connected to a server that then exited (the exit was silent). Changed: `fsd` says why it exits, `volmgr` publishes only after `fsd` reports ready (the `Ready` role) and retries failed starts, and the tests kill their services when they fail. **Not reproduced, so not proven fixed**: if the job flakes again the log now names the exit reason.

## Why it was acceptable

Removes the one race found by reading the code.

## Proper fix, and when

Watch the fail-demo job; if it recurs, read the `fsd: exit N` line.
