## Shortcut

**ASIDs are written to `satp` but not managed** (review finding 5): `asid = pid & 0xffff` repeats after 65536 pids and can be 0 (the kernel's). Safe only because every `satp` write is followed by a global `sfence.vma`; the first "skip the flush" optimisation becomes a cross-process stale-translation bug.

## Why it was acceptable

The flush makes it harmless.

## Proper fix, and when

ASID 0 until an allocator with generations exists, or document the rule and add a negative control. Plan: resolved by SCHED-SMP-SPEC task 18.
