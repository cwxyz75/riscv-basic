## Why it was acceptable

Every one of these reviews' high-severity findings is recorded as fixed; the gaps are medium and low findings and suspicions,
and most reviews say their unfixed findings were "fixed or recorded", which was not always itemised.

## Risk

A real bug among them stays unfixed with nobody looking for it.

## Proper fix, and when

Check each against the code (a fresh-context pass is the cheapest way; read only the finding and the code it names). For each:
close it in the dispositions record by appending a line (fixed in which commit, or accepted and why), or make it its own work
item. Close this item when the list is empty.
