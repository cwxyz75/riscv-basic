title: Kernel stacks and page tables are charged to `Pages`, not to `KernelBytes`
refs: [MM5, T1]
---

MM5 (KERNEL-V1-SPEC 4.7, `work/MM5.md`) restates kernel stacks and page-table frames in bytes under the new kernel-bytes resource, and charges page tables exactly (`PageTable::map` reporting the frames it allocates) instead of by the estimate `length / 4096 / 512 + 4` kept in `table_charge`. Built so far: the resource, object bodies and thread register state. Stacks (`KSTACK_PAGES` per thread) and tables stay on `Pages`, where the job-limit tests of the suite count them (`thread_stacks_are_charged_to_the_job_and_credited_back`, `page_tables_are_charged_to_the_job`, the 64-page hog test), and the table charge is still the estimate.

Why acceptable: both are charged to the job and limited (J1); only the unit and the exactness differ. Risk: a table frame a fault allocates beyond the estimate is uncharged (bounded by the process limits). Proper fix: with the QEMU acceptance test of MM5, move both to `KernelBytes` in one change with the suite tests that read them, and make the table charge exact.
