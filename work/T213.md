title: futex ping-pong does not scale at four harts yet (L14's ratio of 3.0): futex_wait still takes the big lock at trap entry and exit

**What.** L14 (PR in the phase 6c epic) made `futex_wake` run with no big kernel lock (the quiet path of the trap handler, a bucket lock and the scheduler's own locks) and `futex_wait` hold the big lock only to translate the word and queue. The acceptance figure of SCHED-SMP-SPEC task 14, `pair_scaling` futex at least 3.0 at four harts, is **not met**: one run at four harts gave 16.8 us (one pair), 20.4 us (two) and 270 us (four) per round trip over all pairs, a scaling of 0.06 (it was 0.57 before the item, but the one-pair cost then was 200 us; the numbers are from different states of the kernel and the machine).

**Why it waits (updated: L17 is merged, so this is now the next step for the futex path, not blocked).** A blocking `futex_wait` takes the big lock at trap entry (the full path of `trap_handler_inner`), again at each pass of its sleep loop and at the resume after the sleep (`reacquire`), and a third time for `activate_current` at the return. The translation of the word needs the process's address space, which only the big lock protects until L17 parts b and c give it its own lock (`AS`). Only then can `futex_wait` start in the quiet path (no lock at entry, the `AS` lock for the translation, the bucket lock for compare and enqueue, a sleep that holds nothing) and the four takes disappear. Doing it before would put a second, half-peeled entry path in front of the big lock for one call.

**Risk.** The futex numbers of the benchmark stay where they are; nothing is wrong, the call is just not as fast as the plan hoped. The ratio target is carried to L17's and L20's `pair_scaling` checks.

**Proper fix.** After L17 b and c: a quiet entry for `futex_wait` (`lock_free_syscall`), `AS` for the translate and pin, the report and `take_interrupt` without the big lock; then measure `pair_scaling` at four harts against 3.0 and close this item, or file what stops it.

## Plan for the quiet `futex_wait`, as worked out on 2026-11-22 (after the `TT` lock, L19 part, PR 426)

A blocking `futex_wait` with no big lock needs four things, in this order; each is its own PR into the phase 6e epic.
1. **`TT` as a real lock** (done, PR 426): the sleep's switch reads the thread table without the big lock.
2. **A quiet return to user mode** (`activate_current_quiet`): today `activate_current` takes the big lock for `UserState::asids`, `thread_pid` and `procs` (the token of the process and, when its ASID tag is of an old generation, a fresh one). The common case needs none of that: the thread's process and token are cached in its `KThread` (set when it first runs, under the big lock), the allocator's generation is mirrored in an atomic, and a thread whose tag is of the current generation and whose hart already has the token installed (or installs it with no flush: L18) returns with two loads and a store of `cur_pid`/`cur_as`; anything else (a stale tag, a pending stop or kill: `attention`) takes the old path.
3. **A quiet sleep loop** (`wait_deadline_futex_quiet`): `prepare_block_on`, then the condition `!futex_table().contains(key, who) || attention moved`, then `sleep_until_on` and `reschedule()` with no big lock (the thread table is `TT`; `release_all` with depth 0 is a no-op). The reasons a wait ends other than a wake (deadline, kill, interrupt, stop) are not reported from here: the thread leaves the table (a remove that finds it gone was a wake: success) and **restarts the call on the full path** (`sepc` back by 4), which re-reads the word and reports TIMED_OUT, killed or INTERRUPTED as it does today, with the absolute deadline unchanged.
4. **The quiet entry** in `lock_free_syscall`: `light_save`, `user_return_is_quiet`, no suspend states and no copy-on-write sync pending; `cur_pid`/`cur_as` for the `AS` hold and `kmem::mem()` for `futex_wait_resolve` (taking the `AddressSpace`, not `UserState`: its signature changes), the enqueue under the held `AS` (the frame cannot go: every unmap and decommit takes `AS`), a pager-backed or otherwise failing resolve falls back to the full path.
Measure `pair_scaling` futex at four harts against 3.0 after step 4 and close this item or file what stops it.

## Built (2026-11-22, PR on the phase 6e epic): steps 2 to 4

The `TT` lock (PR 426), the quiet return (`activate_current_quiet`: the thread's own `UserBinding` in its `KThread`, the allocator's published generation `Asids::generation_cell`), the quiet sleep (`wait_deadline_futex_quiet`) and the quiet entry (`user::futex_wait_quiet`, from `lock_free_syscall`) are built. Two facts the plan did not have: a pending `thread_interrupt` flag is mirrored in an atomic (`INTERRUPT_FLAGS`, like `SUSPEND_STATES`) and the quiet call takes the full path while one is set; and a quiet wait that ends in the table (deadline, kill, stop, interrupt) leaves the table and **restarts the call on the full path**.
Measured (debug build, `-smp 4`, `OS_BENCH_FULL=1`, one run, a loaded laptop): futex pairs 27 us (one pair), 182 us (two), 111 us (four) per round trip over all pairs, a scaling of 0.25; before: 16.8, 20.4 and 270 us, 0.06 (different machine states: read as 'better, not at 3.0'). What is left between this and 3.0 is not measured: the wake side takes the big lock for a switch it asked for (`futex_wake`'s `need_resched` arm), `kmem::mem()` and the `AS` lock are taken per call, and a debug build on a shared host is noisy. Next: a release-build `pair_scaling` at 4 harts and the lock-wait trace (`TEST-TOOLS.md`) on it before another change.

**Measured (2026-11-24, release build, QEMU TCG at `-smp 4` on a ten-core laptop, one run, `OS_BENCH_FULL=1`):** futex pairs 89.0 us (one pair), 88.9 us (two), 64.5 us (four) per round trip over all pairs: a scaling of 1.38 at four pairs (target 3.0); `channel_call` pairs 250 us, 248 us, 270 us: 0.93. The debug-build figure of 0.25 above is not comparable (another build and machine state). A TCG guest's harts are host threads that the host schedules, so the numbers carry the host's noise; the acceptance figure is the owner's soak run (`make os-latency` for the lock-wait trace).

**Precondition (2026-11-24, owner's decision):** before building the rest of this item, have Fable write a design plan and append it here (a section "Plan (Fable, date)"): what limits futex scaling (the release measurement is 1.38 at four harts against 3.0), the PROCS lock split of L19 with its place in the lock ordering, and the measurements to take first.
