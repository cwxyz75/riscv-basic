A bundle of two small points left by the `iommu-kernel` epic (IOMMU2 to IOMMU7, merged as a2c30d6, #595), filed on 2026-11-26 at the
owner's request so that the `iommu-drivers` epic takes them in one of its PRs.

### 1. Three allows of a panic-finding lint have no `// INVARIANT:` reason

`python3 scripts/kernel_allows.py` names them:

- `kcore/src/sched2.rs:867`
- `ksys/src/create.rs:22`
- `kernel/src/user/mod.rs:118` (`#[allow(clippy::panic)] // (the check's report)`, in `StateOpen::enter`)

KT3 (KERNEL-V1-SPEC 7.4) requires each remaining allow to carry the reason the panic cannot be caused by a program. **Fix:** for
each site, either remove the panic (return an error, or restructure so the lint has nothing to find) or write the `// INVARIANT:`
reason that shows no program can reach it. Then make `scripts/kernel_allows.py` fail on a missing reason if it does not already, so
the count cannot rise again. **Done when** the script reports no site without a reason and that is enforced by the feature gate.

### 2. The ddtp read-back in the IOMMU0 record does not match its own PPN

`docs/records/notes/2026-11-25-iommu-qemu-facts.md` line 67 records a `ddtp` read-back of `0x20c00c02` after writing mode 1LVL
with PPN `0x83000`. `ddtp` holds the mode in bits 3:0 and the PPN from bit 10, so PPN `0x83000` in mode 2 reads back as
`0x20c00002`, which is what `iommucore` (from `riscv-iommu-bits.h`) expects. The extra `0xc00` would be PPN `0x83003`. Either
the record has a typo or the probe wrote a different PPN; the bring-up itself (IOMMU5) works, so nothing depends on the record.
**Fix:** re-observe it once in QEMU 11.1.3 (write 1LVL with a known PPN, read `ddtp` back), and append a dated correction note to
the record (records are append-only: do not edit line 67). **Done when** the note is appended with the observed value.

### Not in this bundle (checked against the spec, 2026-11-26)

- 256 vectors per device: the spec's limit (IOMMU-SPEC 8.3, `kcore::limits::VECTORS_PER_DEVICE`).
- No superpage I/O mappings: a non-goal (IOMMU-SPEC 3, section 14).
