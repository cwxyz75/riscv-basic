## Shortcut

**A channel's WRITABLE signal means "room for some message"**, not "room for this one": a 32 KiB reply to a client whose queue holds 230 KiB is refused with SHOULD_WAIT while WRITABLE is set. Servers must not wait on WRITABLE for backpressure: every service on `rt::service` (memfsd, launcher, devmgr, blkd) now holds the reply and polls on a timer, in one place (slice R); `consoled` is a stream bridge with its own drop-oldest policy.

## Why it was acceptable

Simple signal model (S3a).

## Proper fix, and when

Make WRITABLE precise for a stated message size, or add a "space available" wait with a size, before more servers are written.
