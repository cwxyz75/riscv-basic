## Shortcut

**The launcher serves exactly one client** (`sh`), so a protocol violation by it ends its connection and with it the service (init rebuilds it); since slice R the launcher is on `rt::service` and a client that never reads a reply is held and cut off after a timeout instead of wedging it, but it is still one connection.

## Why it was acceptable

One client in S4; the runtime's violation policy is "close that connection", the same thing with one client.

## Proper fix, and when

Give the launcher more connections (accounts) when a second client exists.
