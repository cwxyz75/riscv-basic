## Shortcut

**The "independent review" is by a model of the same family** as the author; the human does not read the code. Fable-class reviews are expensive and used sparingly (feedback in memory).

## Why it was acceptable

The process rule is the best we have.

## Proper fix, and when

Keep real independent oracles where they exist (host tools, upstream test suites); consider a differently trained reviewer for the highest-risk milestones.
