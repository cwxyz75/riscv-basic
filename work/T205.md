title: L18 leftovers: shootdowns are whole-TLB on every other hart (no `ran_on`, no ASID or range batching), and the ASID-reuse control is unobservable on QEMU
refs: [L18, T64, SC2]
---

L18 part b removed the flush at every address-space switch (translations are tagged by ASID; ASIDs are unique per generation and a hart flushes at a generation change). What it did not build:

1. **`ran_on` and batched shootdowns** (KERNEL-V1-SPEC 5.4, SCHED-SMP-SPEC 3.10): `smp::shootdown` still asks every other online hart for a whole-TLB `sfence.vma` (every ASID), where the spec has an unmap collect up to 64 page ranges and flush only the harts the address space ran on, by ASID and range. Why acceptable: a whole flush on every other hart is a safe superset, TLB2 holds, and at four harts the cost is small. Risk: the cost grows with the harts (SC2 measures it at 16 and 32).
2. **The negative control** `fault-asid-reuse-no-flush` (a feature of the kernel) skips a hart's flush at a generation change and counts the ASID field as one bit, but QEMU drops its translations whenever `satp` is written, so no boot can show a stale translation under a reused ASID: the host model (`kcore/tests/asid_model.rs`, mutation-checked) is the oracle, and the control is not run by a soak target. Correctness on hardware is by review only (PL7 is in the same position).

Proper fix: 1 with SC2 (the `ran_on` mask is the per-address-space hart set, cleared when a hart goes offline: PM1); 2 on a machine that implements ASIDs in its TLB (real hardware, or an emulator that does).
