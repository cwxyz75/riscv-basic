## Shortcut

**TLB shootdowns flush everything on every other hart** (SBI `remote_sfence_vma` with no range or ASID) on each unmap and teardown, and run synchronously under the big kernel lock. Correct but coarse.

## Why it was acceptable

Few harts, short regions.

## Proper fix, and when

Range/ASID shootdowns, tracking which harts ran a given address space, batching (S5 rung 3 or later). Plan: resolved by SCHED-SMP-SPEC task 18.
