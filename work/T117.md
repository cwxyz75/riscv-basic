## Shortcut

**Open findings of the doofs review** (`reviews/2026-11-16-doofs-slice.md`; findings 1 to 7, 9 and 12 are fixed in the review-fixes PR):

- (8) fixed in `fsd` (a renamed symlink is re-checked against the connection root; `memfs` and `doofs` themselves still do not know the root)
- (10) `make_index`, truncate, unlink and mount read whole record sets into memory (bounded by the volume, not streamed)
- (11) fixed: `fsd` answers TooBig
- (14) `fsck` does not check the next-id record, name record against entry, or an unnamed node with `links = 2`
- (15) `MakeIndex`/`Query` leak a little across subtrees (a `TooBig` oracle, timing, index-slot exhaustion)
- (16) small `fsd` items (`changed()` before the rights check, `SYMLINK` lets a `CREATE`-only connection probe names, the driver's `read_only` flag is ignored)
- (17) `Tree` insert/delete are atomic on error only inside a pool transaction (`doofs` always uses one; `fsfmt` does not say so). Also still true: the independent checker (T113).

## Why it was acceptable

None breaks the invariants on a healthy volume;

- (8) and
- (11) are the ones a hostile client can reach.

## Proper fix, and when

- (8) and
- (11) next; the rest with the `volmgr` slice.
