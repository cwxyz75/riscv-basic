title: MM7 left out: per-job region counts, and the small deviations from the spec's process_map_info
refs: [MM7, PL8]
---

MM7 (KERNEL-V1-SPEC 3.5, 4.5) is built except (2026-11-21; the C library's `mmap`/`munmap`/`mprotect`, the protect-versus-sibling negative control, the ANYWHERE flag and its eight-random-tries placement were done in the epic):

1. **The region count per job** (waits for PL8): `MAX_REGIONS` (16,384) is the per-address-space limit; the spec has a per-job `regions` level, which needs the object registry rebuilt (PL8).
2. **`process_map_info`** writes six words and the VMO *store* id, not the spec's seventh word and a VMO koid: a region does not know its VMO object, and the spec does not say what the seventh word (`flags`) holds. The id is global and stable for a VMO's life: an INSPECT holder of a process can learn the ids of VMOs it has no handle to (never a capability). Fix when the spec names the word: record the object id in the region.
3. **ANYWHERE keeps no guard gap** and can land flush against the main stack's top if the child's stack is mapped after it.
4. **Merging at map time** makes `process_unmap(vaddr, 0)` remove a merged mapping whole (documented in SYSCALLS); and a thread's spawn stack is credited and freed by its region start (`free_spawn_stack`): a process that protects or range-unmaps the first page of its own thread stack leaves the rest mapped until process exit (a self-inflicted leak, not a bypass).
5. **Range operations have no length bound** (waits for L19): one `process_protect` or range `process_unmap` walks every page of every region in the range under the big lock; chunked and preemptible with L19.
6. **A VMO mapped writable at one address and executable at another** is not synchronised per frame (PL7 syncs when a page becomes executable); M9's "never writable and executable at once" holds per mapping, not per frame.
7. **`PROT_NONE`** is not available to the C library: a mapping always reads (guard pages: ENOSYS).
8. **The page-table charge of a split mapping** is kept per `process_map` (`Process::charge_end`) and credited when no region is left in that span: it can stay higher than the tables that are left, never lower.
