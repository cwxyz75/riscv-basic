## Shortcut

**`clockd` v0** (step C1):

- (1) no `Set` and no network time (the model has `Synced`, nothing sets it)
- (2) fixed: `volmgr` gives each `fsd` a registry view that allows `clock`, and `fsd` keeps an offset to the monotonic clock re-read every 30 s (checked in QEMU through the `mtime` index); a volume started without a clock service uses time since boot
- (3) the RTC is re-read every 60 s and each re-read is a `sync` (a correction of the RTC is slewed at half speed; a large step forward is applied at once)
- (4) the RTC page is a kernel device window (`abi::layout::DEVICE_WINDOWS`), hard-coded like the virtio slots (T41): a board with its RTC elsewhere needs the window from the device tree
- (5) no leap seconds or time zones (UTC nanoseconds only)
- (6) a clock with no RTC counts from boot, so file times after a reboot go backwards (no persisted last-known time).

## Why it was acceptable

Enough for a QEMU machine and the tests.

## Proper fix, and when

- (2) with 5b
- (4) with T41
- (6) a persisted last-known time when a board without an RTC matters.
