## Shortcut

**Gaps in the S4d/S4e tests**: no program exits early on a closed pipe (exit 141 untested); whole-stack restart after a service death and a bad halt message are untested; consoled's NoMemory retry and delivery cap are untested.

## Why it was acceptable

Needs fault injection (a service that dies on cue, an exhausted IPC budget) that does not exist yet.

## Proper fix, and when

A `die` hook in a test build of memfsd and a budget-hog program.
