## Shortcut

**Task 10 stand-ins** (Fable review of klock and the park protocol):

- (1) done by L11: the steps run under `RQ` locks (PRs 262 to 264)
- (2) `HartRq` has `prepare_block` but no `cancel_block`
- (3) the lock-order checker records locks per hart only for classes that have a lock in the kernel (LEGACY, HEAP, UART): the others are covered by the pure pair table, and the kernel test probes ten pairs, not every adjacent pair of 3.4
- (4) `Machine::hart` is `u32` here, `usize` in the spec text
- (5) `Held` is lenient about a release of an unheld reentrant lock (the BKL's hand-overs) and still pushes a violating lock in the test mode
- (6) the park model has one waiter and two identical wakers (no separate timer, no second wait round) and drives `Park` through a hand-copied protocol, not `Sched::wake_on`

## Why it was acceptable

None is reachable under the big lock

## Proper fix, and when

With task 11: use `Park` inside `Sched`, give the model a real timer waker and a second wait, and add the per-class kernel locks to the order test as they appear
