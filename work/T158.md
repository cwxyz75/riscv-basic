## Shortcut

`scripts/s4d_console_test.py` writes 500,000 bytes to the console server and expects some to be dropped (the queue bound). On CI's soak job `AIA, RVA23, polling, no disk, serial` it failed on PR 238 (first run) and PR 250 and passed on re-runs; locally it passes. A fast runner may drain the flood as fast as it arrives, so nothing is dropped: the check depends on the relative speed of the writer and the reader.

## Why it was acceptable

A re-run passes; the bound itself is unit-tested in `proto::tty`.

## Proper fix, and when

Make the check deterministic: stop the reader (hold the terminal's output) while writing, or assert on the server's counters instead of on dropped bytes. Soon (it reddens soak runs).

## Note (2026-11-18, plan 4: the v1 kernel)

OD140 and `docs/reference/TESTING.md` rule 8: this is a defect until its cause is known, not a flake to re-run. Plan 4 phase 1 takes it with KT2's record and repeat tools: establish the pass rate over 20 runs, capture a failing run, find the cause, fix the code or the test, and show the rate after (20 of 20).
