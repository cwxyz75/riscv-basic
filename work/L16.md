## Note (2026-11-18, plan 4: the v1 kernel)

Amended by KERNEL-V1-SPEC 4.3 and 4.7. (1) `FRAME` wraps the buddy allocator of MM2; the per-hart frame caches are as specified. (2) `VMO` is **one lock per VMO family**, not one lock for the store: the store itself (id to family) is a small table under a lock of the same class taken only to look a family up and never while a family lock is held. Before MM8 a family is a single VMO. (3) **Page reservations**: an address space holds up to 64 prepaid pages from its job; `page_for` takes from the reservation and refills in batches under `JOBS`; teardown and a 100 ms idle return the remainder. J1 counts reservations as usage; the ledger model is extended with them. Plan 4 (`WORK-PLAN.md`) orders this item and carries the owner's go-ahead for it; the sentence above about waiting for a go-ahead and the ordering gate no longer applies (the order is the plan's, changed only on the evidence of the lock profile, recorded as a decision).
