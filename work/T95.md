## Shortcut

**Who pays for the filesystem cache and space is a first cut** (F5): disk space is charged to persistent quota domains on directories, but the block cache is charged to `fsd`'s own job under a fixed cap, so one client can evict or crowd out another's cached data and no client pays for the memory it causes.

## Why it was acceptable

Per-client attribution of shared cached blocks needs pager-backed VMOs (Q4); not available in v1.

## Proper fix, and when

Revisit with Q4 and slice R accounts: charge cached pages to the clients that caused them (or to the quota domain), and consider per-domain cache shares.
