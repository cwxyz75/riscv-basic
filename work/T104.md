## Shortcut

**Symlink and hard-link policy is not built or tested yet** (OD17 amended 2026-11-15): symlinks are relative-only and resolved against the connection they were found through, absolute targets are refused, and there are no hard links. Nothing implements symlinks yet: `memfs`, `proto::file` and the libc have no `symlink`/`readlink` (checked by grep, 2026-11-15), so the rule is a design constraint on the first implementation, not a fix to existing code. Ported software that expects absolute links (`/bin/sh -> busybox`, `/usr/lib/...`) or `link` will get errors.

## Why it was acceptable

The rule keeps links from carrying authority; the policy is decided, the enforcement is part of slice F step 0 and the libc work.

## Proper fix, and when

Enforce in `memfs` and the libc first (a golden test with a hostile planted link, a loop, an absolute target); then in doofs (FS-SPEC check 9); revisit if S11 ports need absolute links, with a namespace-level alias as the preferred answer.
