## Shortcut

**`nettcp` step 2 shortcuts** (S9 step 2):

- (1) the persist probe is one byte sent past the window that then rides the ordinary retransmission timer, so after the first probe the interval is the RTO's backoff (200 ms doubling) and not a separate persist timer capped at 60 s apart from the first
- (2) SYN and SYN-ACK retransmissions follow the fixed 1, 2, 4, 8 s schedule (retransmissions at 1, 3, 7 s; failure at 15 s; SYN-ACK at 1, 3 s; dropped at 7 s), a reading of S9-SPEC 5.5's "after 1, 2, 4 and 8 s" that the spec should state
- (3) [resolved in step 3: RFC 5681 and NewReno are in]
- (4) challenge ACKs use one shared limiter the caller owns, and the RST-for-no-connection limiter is the caller's too.

## Why it was acceptable

Host-only crate, covered by 40 lossy-link seeds and the packet scripts; nothing boots it yet.

## Risk

A real peer's behaviour is untested until step 4 (and H15, the smoltcp peer, is T86: the crate download was refused).

## Proper fix, and when

Step 4.
