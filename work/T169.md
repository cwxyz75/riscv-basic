title: L13 leftovers: equal-priority balance only at timer events, timer slack not settable from user mode, no RT window entry

## Shortcut

L13b (no tick) leaves: (1) `Sched2::balance` (the share-out of equal-priority queues, T107 (3)) ran at every tick and now runs at timer events only (a slice end or a sleeper's deadline on that hart), so a lopsided queue of equals on a hart that has no event stays lopsided until a thread blocks or the hart's slice rotates; (2) the thread's `timer_slack` (default 50 µs normal, 0 RT) has no user-mode setter (the reserved flags of `thread_set_sched`, SCHED-SMP-SPEC 4.11); (3) the RT window boundary timer entry (4.11) does not exist because RT budgets are not enforced yet; (4) hart 0's clock and the scheduler's first charge start at the first event (benign).

## Why acceptable

The suite and the smoke gate pass; (1) only affects fairness among equal-priority threads on busy harts, (2) and (3) are features nothing uses yet.

## Proper fix, and when

(1) give an overloaded hart a slice-end action (balance at the slice end that already exists, and on a mark), with a test on the pure model that equal queues converge without ticks; (2) with the SC/RT syscall work; (3) with RT enforcement. Before L14.
