## L12a built (2026-11-19): the lock is released at a switch (G4)

`reschedule` releases the big lock completely before `context_switch` (`bkl::release_all`, the depth stays on the thread's stack) and the thread that resumes takes it back to that depth (`bkl::reacquire`); a new thread locks in its bootstrap. The lock no longer follows the thread (K3 rewritten; `KThread.bkl_depth` and `bkl::swap_depth` are gone). What the lock used to cover is now the scheduler's transit rule (TR1), widened: a thread that blocked, slept or exited is *in transit* on its hart (`Sched2::displaced` is called by `block_on`, `sleep_until_on` and `exit_on` with the thread they leave, whatever its state) until the incoming thread's `switch_done` (the scheduler half of `finish_switch`, run before it waits for the lock), so no other hart can wake-and-run it, pull it or reap it before its context is saved. A wake of a blocked thread that is still in transit queues it on the hart that is executing it (every other hart's own queue could be served before the switch is done); `switched` marks that hart when such a thread outranks what it runs next; `join` waits for an exited thread's transit to end; the audit's transit rule no longer requires state Runnable. Checked by: `kcore/tests/sched_transit_g4.rs` (deterministic: a blocked, slept or exited thread is not run or reaped before its switch is done; mutation, the old Runnable-only transit, is caught by two of three), the shuttle model's new G4 mode (`rq_concurrency`: the lock dropped before each switch is finished, with yields in the switch window; it passes but does not by itself catch the transit mutation: the window needs a wake, a placement and a settle inside it), the reference walks (they reap only after the switch is done), `os-repeat os-ktest-4` 8 of 8, and the k boots at 1, 2 and 4 harts and the release kernel. Left: **L12b** the timer tick and the reschedule IPI that interrupt user mode take no big lock when the hart need not switch (an *attention epoch* bumped at the kill and stop sites, a per-hart seen epoch, FP state not dirty, no heap service owed); **L12c** `thread_yield`, `clock_get` and pure sleeps without the lock; T163 (the dead list at safe points) and T164 (stack poison) with them.

Review of L12a (fresh-context Fable, 2026-11-19): one HIGH, fixed: `reap_prev` runs after the lock is taken again, which can wait, so `prev` can be stale; an exited detached thread could be reaped while it was in transit on another hart (use-after-free of its stack and context); `reap_if_dead` now skips a thread in transit (the thread that replaces it on its hart reaps it after that switch is done). Also fixed: the slept test was vacuous (now wakes the sleeper from the other hart), the audit again requires a Runnable thread in transit to be queued, stale comments. Not fixed (tidy T165): `sched2_kernel_walk` still models K3 (`bkl_barrier`), so big-lock operations never run inside a switch window there, and the shuttle G4 mode has no reacquire-then-reap-`prev` step.
