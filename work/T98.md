## Shortcut

**S9 step 4b review findings not fixed** (Fable, `reviews/2026-11-15-s9-step4b.md`; findings 1 to 4 and 7 are fixed):

- (5) the orphan cap of 32 is global, so one client can make others' draining connections be reset
- (6) every service connection is a fresh account, so several can together reserve the whole 4 MiB pool (no per-job limit on opening the name)
- (8) the stream pass serves a round-robin window of 32 over all entries, not ready streams (about 80 ms latency at 256 streams)
- (9) socket bindings are not unbound when a stream ends (they go with the handle)
- (10) a second notice while one is held closes the channel (for a listener, the listener)
- (11) an abort while connecting is reported as refused, `ClearConfig` on a connecting stream is not reported as link-lost, a second `Connect` answers `CONNECT_FAILED`
- (12) `tcp_connect` accepts broadcast, multicast, zero and port-0 destinations
- (13) the listener address is unchecked and ports are first come first served.

## Why it was acceptable

None changes the safety properties (handles, accounting, boundedness) the review checked.

## Risk

- (5) and
- (6) let one program degrade the others'
- (8) throughput at many streams.

## Proper fix, and when

Step 8 (churn and soak), and the launcher quota work (6).
