title: Exited detached threads are freed inside finish_switch under the big lock, not from a per-hart dead list at a safe point
---

## Shortcut

L11c part 1 (PR 262) frees an exited detached thread in `finish_switch` (`reap_if_dead`), with interrupts off and the big kernel lock held, where SCHED-SMP-SPEC 3.6 has `finish_switch` move it to `PerHart.dead` and the hart reap at a safe point (`preempt_point()`, return to user, idle) with no lock held, plus `reap_quiesce()` for leak oracles. Review (Fable, 2026-11-19): the work (stack scan `note_stack_use`, map remove, heap and stack frees, two scheduler lock takes) is now charged to whichever thread resumes, possibly an RT thread, where the reaper thread paid it before. The safety argument depends on the big lock covering exit through `finish_switch`; `join` of a joined thread relies on the same.

## Why acceptable

The lock follows the thread until L12, so no reap can race a switch; the leak tests pass. The work is the same as the reaper's.

## Proper fix, and when

L12 (the scheduler leaves the big lock): per-hart `dead` list filled by `finish_switch`, drained at the safe points, `reap_quiesce()`, and `join` using the same hand-over; check B3 wake latency before and after. Do it with L12.
