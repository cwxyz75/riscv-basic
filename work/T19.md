## Shortcut

**The shell's `PATH` is a fixed `/bin`**; commands are looked up by a plain name; programs are only the boot image.

## Why it was acceptable

Boot image is the only program source.

## Proper fix, and when

Manifest-driven search paths and a package/installer story (S10).
