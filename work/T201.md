title: Page reservations are returned only at teardown, not after 100 ms of idleness; a reservation counts in `job_info`
refs: [L16, MM5]
---

L16 part d (`kcore::aspace::Reservation`): an address space prepays pages from its job for the fault path (up to 64 at a time, an eighth of the job's room at most, one at least, so a prepayment near a limit cannot starve the job's other users: a 64-page job prepays 8). The prepaid pages count as the job's usage (J1) and come back when the address space is destroyed. KERNEL-V1-SPEC 4.7 also has them returned after 100 ms of idleness. Not built: it needs a timer or a safe-point hook that visits the address spaces that have prepaid pages, which L17's per-process locks give for free (a per-process housekeeping step).

Consequences until then: `job_info` reports usage including the prepaid pages of live address spaces (up to 64 per process of the job, fewer near the limit), so a job's usage can read a little above what its VMOs hold while its processes run; the hog and limit tests tolerate it because the batch shrinks with the room. Proper fix: the idle return with L17, and `job_info` may then report committed and prepaid separately. Risk: a job with many idle processes can have up to 64 pages each prepaid (bounded by an eighth of its room per refill); a process with nothing to fault holds none.
