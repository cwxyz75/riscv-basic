title: L16 part b's stand-ins: one `VMO` lock for the whole store, and `&'static mut` fields on the lock bundles
refs: [L16, T196]

L16 part b (PR 350) made the VMO store, the job ledger and the frame database three real locks. Two things differ from the end state.

1. **One `VMO` lock for the store, not one per family** (KERNEL-V1-SPEC 4.3, the note in `work/L16.md`). Before MM8 a family is one VMO, so a store lock is the same thing in effect; the store/family split belongs with MM8 or L17, whichever needs two VMOs locked at once. Why acceptable: the big lock still serialises every taker. Risk: none until the big lock is peeled.
2. **`kmem::Kmem`, `Frames` and `JobsRef` have public `&'static mut` fields** (found by the review of PR 350): safe code can move a reference out past its guard (`kmem::jobs().jobs` returned from a function). No site does it; every site is a statement temporary or a named guard. Proper fix: a newtype `Ref<T>` that derefs with the lifetime of the bundle, or private fields and a `parts(&mut self)` accessor, with the call sites (about 100) rewritten. Do it before the big lock goes (L20), when a misuse would stop being masked.
