**What.** Seen once on 2026-11-23 (epic/phase-12 gate, `ktest 4 harts`): `a_thread_made_by_the_parent_faults_or_exits` failed `the process ended: got -12, want 0` (the `wait_ended` of 1000 ticks timed out, either the null-load fault or the `exit(42)` child). Everything else in the shard passed; it failed on every gate run from the squash of AB7-AB9 into the epic (2026-11-23, three runs) and never locally, then passed on the run after PR 494 (the non-reentrant object lock), with a diagnostic in place that prints the process and thread state if it times out again. Kept open until it has passed on a few more gate runs.
**Likely cause.** A slow teardown on a loaded runner, or a lost wake of the TERMINATED waiter when a sibling thread is killed by the fault on another hart.
**Why acceptable.** One occurrence, development only.
**Proper fix.** Repeat the test at four harts (`make os-repeat`); if it reproduces, find the stuck thread with the starved-thread panic; otherwise widen the wait.
