## Shortcut

**The random service (S9 step 5) leftovers**:

- (1) the secret reaches `netd` in its startup **environment** (`SECRET=<hex>`, derived by HKDF-SHA256 from the generator), not in a read-only VMO as S9-SPEC 5.10 said: the startup message is private to the child and `netsup` keeps it across restarts, so the stack's restart contract (same secret, ISNs never go backwards) holds
- (2) with no entropy device or no `rngd`, `netsup` falls back to a hash of the clock and says so (`SECRET_SRC=standin`, `##NETSUP tcp secret from stand-in`): guessable by anyone who watches the boot, tolerable under slirp, to be removed once every machine has a device
- (3) `rngd` polls the device (no interrupt), reseeds from it every 1 MiB served, and is started only by the net tests' `start_full` and by whoever boots the full stack: `init` does not start it yet
- (4) the generator is `rngcore::Drbg` (ChaCha20, fast key erasure); no other entropy is mixed in (no device-tree `rng-seed`, no timing jitter): a second source is an easy addition
- (5) `rngd` has no per-client rate limit beyond its account's inflight limit
- (6) there is no `getrandom` in the C library yet (S9 step 6)
- (7) review findings not fixed (`reviews/2026-11-15-s9-step5.md`): one client can hold the 64 connections of the service and deny it to others (no idle eviction); `rngd` claims the device at start and does not retry a `Busy` or late reply, and does not restart or republish itself; a `devmgr` connection is all-or-nothing (a client could claim any device); the fallback secret is a hash of the clock and is used after 15 s without `rngd`; the suite test passes on `NOT_SEEDED` and has no negative controls.

## Why it was acceptable

The primitives are checked against their RFC vectors (`rngcore/tests`), the service by a QEMU test and the harness requires `netd: tcp secret from rngd`.

## Risk

- (2) is a real weakness on a machine with no device
- (3) a boot that brings up the network some other way has no secret from rngd.

## Proper fix, and when

- (3) and
- (6) with step 6; the rest with step 8.
