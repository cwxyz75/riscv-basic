## Shortcut

**`ran_on` is sticky**: a hart that ever ran an address space gets its shootdowns until the process dies

## Why it was acceptable

Correct; shootdowns are by ASID and range, so cheap

## Proper fix, and when

Clear a hart's bit with a local ASID flush when it has not run the space for a while
