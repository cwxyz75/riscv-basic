**Q13: the recovery policy's format and menu** (section 10.1): the configuration keys, whether a mechanism can require a second party or a delay, and whether the policy is changeable only by an authenticated session or also from the console. The first built mechanism is the physical console only.

Source: USER-VIEW-SPEC section 15.
