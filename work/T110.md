## Shortcut

**`fsfmt` B+tree leftovers** (filesystem step 1):

- (1) delete merges only a small child (under a quarter block) with a neighbour that fits, no borrowing, so a tree can stay sparse (correct, not space-optimal)
- (2) internal bounds are lower bounds and are not tightened on delete (routing stays right, `verify` checks the invariant)
- (3) `range` collects into a `Vec` (no streaming cursor; the server pages with the 1000 cap)
- (4) the pool's free list is in memory only, persisted by the step-2 format
- (5) one B+tree per key space, with no shared-block (clone) reference counts yet.

## Why it was acceptable

Correct under the random walk and `verify`; none is reachable from a client.

## Proper fix, and when

- (1) if fsck shows bloat
- (3) with the query path at step 6
- (4) at step 2.
