## Shortcut

**The reachability sweep runs whole under the object lock**: O(live objects) with interrupts off, the known worst case of the latency budget

## Why it was acceptable

Rare (process death, pressure)

## Proper fix, and when

Incremental marking with an epoch, or a low-priority sweeper thread (task 19 decides)
