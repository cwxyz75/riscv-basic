**What.** Soak re-run of 45526124119 (2026-11-25), job `smp`, `make os-latency` (the latency-trace kernel, four harts): `thread_stacks_are_charged_to_the_job_and_credited_back` failed at its last step, `process_kill(p, 3)` returned `BadState` ("kill: got Some(BadState), want Some(Ok)"). The child runs `crash.rs` mode 16 (block forever in `object_wait` on its bootstrap channel, `return 9` if the wait ends), and the test starts a second thread on the same entry, so the process should be alive until killed. Not reproduced: three local `make os-latency` boots passed that test, and the same boot passed in the first attempt of the soak and in the epic smoke.

**Why acceptable.** One occurrence on a loaded runner. It may be the child ending by itself (the bootstrap wait returned, so `return 9` ended the process), the process being ended by the job, or a kill that raced a thread exit. Which, is unknown.

**Risk.** A wait that returns early on the bootstrap channel, or a kill that sees a live process as ended, would be a kernel fault.

**Proper fix.** On a recurrence, read the failing-boot log: the process's exit code (9 means the wait ended by itself), the thread's signals and `process_info` at the failure. Make the test report them (it reports its detail for the earlier steps, not for the kill). If it never recurs in three soaks, close it as load noise.
