MM6 (KERNEL-V1-SPEC 3.5, 4.3) is built except what waits for work that comes later (2026-11-21; the rest, sizes in bytes, the decommit negative control, the page-table walk that skips absent tables and the fuzz coverage, was done in the epic):

1. **No cap** (waits for phase 6c: L19, PL8): `MAX_VMO_PAGES` (16,384 pages) and `MAX_REGION_PAGES` (1 GiB) stay. Removing them needs region unmap and teardown chunked with preemption (a 2 GiB region is half a million page-table entries cleared inside one big-lock section), which is L19 (the big lock's end); PL8 removes the constants. The QEMU acceptance at `-m 4G` (a 2 GiB VMO sparsely touched) comes with it.
2. **A VMO's list of its mappings** (waits for phase 6c: L17 b/c, the address-space locks): `vmo_decommit` finds the mappings by walking every process's regions (`AddressSpace::unmap_vmo_pages`: O(processes times regions), now skipping absent page tables) instead of the spec's per-VMO list ordered by process id with one address-space lock at a time. Acceptable until then: the big lock serialises it and processes are few.
3. **`fault_lookup`/`fault_map` and a decommit** (closed by MM9: a pager wait does not use the three-step fault across the wait; it retries `handle_fault_mapped` from the top once the page is supplied, so no frame found before the wait is mapped after it. The split stays for a caller that does let go between the steps, which none does: a decommit still does not bump `Region::gen`, and a future caller of the phases must add a VMO generation first).
4. **The allocator returning memory with decommit** (a design decision, not built): `rt`'s heap commits each chunk when it grows (slice V: exhaustion is a failed allocation, never a fault that ends the program) and closes the VMO handle; giving freed pages back with `vmo_decommit` would turn a later reuse into a fault that can end the program. It needs either a recommit on reuse (the heap keeps the VMO handles) or a policy that a heap may fault; the owner's call when a program needs it.

Equivalent mutants of the model test (`vmo_ops_model.rs`): skipping the clip of a decommit range to its region's end over-unmaps pages a later fault maps again.

**Precondition (2026-11-24, owner's decision):** before building the rest of this item, have Fable write a design plan and append it here (a section "Plan (Fable, date)"): the VMO size cap and per-VMO mapping lists, what they change in the frame accounting guarantees, the committed-page map's sparse cost and the VMO id generation wrap, and the order of the PRs.

## Plan (Fable, 2026-11-24)

### 0. What the caps hide today

Every one of these is O(range) or O(processes) in one locked, interrupts-off section: `Vmos::decommit` (`for page in first..end`, and one hole per page of an inherited window), `Vmos::is_committed` (V1 at `process_map` and clone), `Vmos::release` (`drain_pfns` of every committed page at the last close), `AddressSpace::unmap_region` and `write_protect_vmo` (`for p in 0..pages`), `destroy_user_chunk` (its budget counts virtual pages, so 2 GiB sparse is 8,192 sections), teardown's `decommit` loop over `first..pages`, `PageMap` (outer vector sized by the highest page: one page at 2 GiB is 196 KiB, uncharged), and `AsLocks::all` for a decommit (every `AS` lock at once). Three `// INVARIANT:` comments (`aspace.rs` 1278, 1890; `pagemap.rs` 38) justify unchecked arithmetic by the caps.

### 1. Data structures (all in `kcore`, `forbid(unsafe_code)`)

- **`PageMap`**: `FMap<u64, Leaf>` keyed by `page / 64`; `Leaf` is `Pages(FBox<[u64; 64]>, count: u8)` or `AllHole` (a whole chunk of explicit holes, no array). A leaf whose count reaches 0 is freed (removal never allocates). `insert`/`insert_hole` are class R (`reserve(1)` + `FBox::try_new`, `NoMemory`, nothing changed). New allocation-free walks: `next_entry_at_or_after` (exists), `count_frames_in(first, end)`, `take_frames_in(first, end, &mut [Pfn; 64]) -> (n, next)`. Cost: 528 bytes per touched chunk, **charged to the VMO's job as `Resource::KernelBytes`** when the leaf is made and credited when it goes. Dense: 0.2% of the data; worst (one page per chunk): 13%. T177's "sparse cost" sub-item closes with this.
- **`Vmo::maps: FMap<(u64, u64), MapRef>`**, key (process id, region base), `MapRef { pages, vmo_page, job: JobId }`. `AddressSpace` gains `owner: u64` (set when the cell is armed). Maintained under `AS(owner)` + `VMO` by `map_region_max` (`maps.reserve(1)` beside `regions.reserve(1)`, before anything changes), `split_at` (one more reserve each), `unmap_region`, `merge_near`, `destroy_user_chunk` (`rekey`). `Vmo::refs` stays (L15).
- **`Vmo::busy: Option<(u64, u64)>`**: the page range a decommit or shrink is working on. **`Vmos::detached: u64`**: frames taken out of a page index and not yet freed. **`VmoSlot::dying_next`**: an intrusive list of VMOs whose last reference went with more than 64 committed pages.
- **`Region::dying: bool`**: a region being unmapped in chunks; a fault on it is `NoRegion`, `find_gap` and `map_region` see it as occupied, `map_info`, protect and other unmaps skip it.

**ML1** (new invariant): `v.maps` has `(pid, base) -> (pages, vmo_page)` exactly when the address space of `pid` has a region at `base` on `v` with those values; `refs == creator + maps.len() + children`. Every writer holds both locks, so a holder of either sees its side stable. Checked by `Vmos::audit_maps(spaces)` after every model step.

### 2. Chunked operations

Budget per locked section: 64 PTEs cleared or frames freed, **and** 2,048 page-table entries scanned (`PageTable::next_mapped_budgeted`: a present but empty leaf table costs 512 reads). Between sections: the switch point `teardown_memory` uses (`irq::without(|| {})`). Each section does its shootdown before its locks go (TLB2).

- **`vmo_decommit` / shrink** (new `vmo_range_call` in `kernel/src/user/calls.rs`): (0) `OBJ`: resolve the handle, rights, then `VMO`: kind checks, V4 from `maps` (a `MapRef.job` other than the VMO's, in it and every node below), set `busy`, for a shrink set `pages = new` now; `retain`. `OBJ` released. (1) Loop: **A** `kmem::mem()`: `take_frames_in` moves up to 64 frames to a batch on the kernel stack (`detached += n`; holes in an inherited window): from here a fault sees zeros. **B** for each process id above the cursor in `maps` of the VMO and its descendants, up to the highest id seen at A: `mem()` to read the next id, release; `OBJ` to clone the `Arc<AsCell>`, release; `AsCell::hold`, check the cell's pid, `mem()`, clear the PTEs of the batch's span by `next_mapped`, shoot down, release both. **C** `mem()`: drop the batch's references, credit the job, `detached -= n`. (2) clear `busy`, `release`. One `AS` at a time, taken with nothing held.
- **Why a skipped or late mapping is safe**: a mapping holds its own frame reference, so a frame is never reused under a live PTE (M1); a fault is one hold of `AS` + `VMO` (AS2; item 3's phases are still not split), so every PTE of a batch frame existed before A and its mapping is in the list; a process gone from the table cannot run and its teardown drops the references. A mapping made after A cannot reach a detached frame.
- **`process_unmap`**: split at the edges and mark the regions `dying` in the first section, then clear in budgeted sections, dropping each region, its `maps` entry and its VMO reference with its last page. **`process_protect`**: regions get their new permissions in the first section, the PTEs follow in budgeted sections (M9: all before the call returns). `write_protect_vmo` (SNAPSHOT) walks `maps` with `next_mapped` instead of every page, in budgeted sections.
- **Teardown**: `destroy_user_chunk` uses `next_mapped_budgeted`; the VMO loop uses `take_frames_in`.
- **Last close**: `Vmos::release` frees at most 64 pages inline; more puts the VMO on the dying list and sets `DRAIN_PENDING`; the syscall tail and teardown run `Vmos::drain_dying(64)` between switch points until the list is empty, before the caller returns to user mode.

### 3. Lock order (INVARIANTS K5, AS1)

Sequences: `OBJ > VMO` (step 0); `VMO,JOBS,FRAME` alone; `OBJ` alone; `AS(pid) > VMO,JOBS,FRAME` (AS1: the walker reads the next pid under `VMO`, **lets go**, then takes `AS`). Never two `AS`; never `AS` then `OBJ`; nothing held at a switch point. Ascending process id is not needed against deadlock; it makes the cursor resumable and the walk finite (ids are never reused, CNT1; the bound taken at A stops a walk chasing new processes).

### 4. Frame accounting: what changes

- J1/COW2 formula becomes: pages charged == frames in page indexes + `detached` + prepaid reservations; kernel bytes charged include index leaves. Exact at every lock release.
- V2 is untouched (`vmo_commit`, `MAX_COMMIT_PAGES` stay). V4 is checked at step 0 and **kept true during the call by `busy`**: a foreign-job `process_map`/clone over a busy range is refused.
- A 2 GiB sparse mapping costs: one region entry and one `maps` entry; nothing per untouched page; per touched page the frame (job pages, through the reservation), page tables charged exactly as today (worst 4 MiB for one touch per 2 MiB), and the index leaf.
- Credit for a large last close or decommit arrives in chunks, complete before the caller is back in user mode.
- M3 unchanged; a process being torn down may still hold frames a decommit has credited (as today).

### 5. Error behaviour (SYSCALLS.md, changed in PR 4)

- `vmo_create`, `pager_create_vmo`, `vmo_set_size`: INVALID_ARGS above the user half (`abi::user::TOP` bytes), not above 64 MiB. `process_map`: no 1 GiB refusal. `abi::limits::MAX_VMO_PAGES` and `kcore::aspace::MAX_REGION_PAGES` are deleted; arithmetic uses checked operations.
- `vmo_decommit`, `vmo_set_size`: **SHOULD_WAIT** when the VMO is busy with another range call (also `vmo_create_child` and `pager_supply` on it); **NO_MEMORY is no longer all or nothing** in a member of a family (holes are made chunk by chunk: the pages before the failure are gone; call again); not atomic against a concurrent `vmo_write` to the range (pages written behind the cursor stay).
- `process_unmap`: every failure is before the first page goes; a sibling's fault in the range during the call is an exception.

### 6. T177 rules

Class R: `PageMap` leaves, `maps.reserve`, hole making. Class P: the `maps` entry after its reserve, the dying list link, the batch (stack). No `Vec` or `BTreeMap` growth is added; `decommit`'s `made` vector goes. Each new R site gets a scenario in `ksys/tests/allocfail.rs` (map, split, sparse commit, decommit in a child); the docs-check site count is updated.
