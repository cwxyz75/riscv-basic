## Shortcut

**Mounting shortcuts** (proposed in `FS-SPEC.md` section 6, not built): volumes are identified by uuid and named by `/etc/mounts`, but grants are `/etc/trusted` entries until the S10 manifest exists, so the grant side is coarse (a trusted program, not a per-program list); `volmgr` probes untrusted superblocks (must stay panic-free: check 12); a cloned disk is refused, not disambiguated; no formatting from inside the OS; no hot removal or partition tables; `/` stays the boot image; `/etc/mounts` is read at program start, not watched.

## Why it was acceptable

Fits a fixed QEMU disk set and a confined default.

## Proper fix, and when

The S10 manifest for per-program grants; an in-OS `mkfs` behind its own right; a watched table.
