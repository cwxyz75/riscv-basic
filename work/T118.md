## Shortcut

**`volmgr` v0** (steps 5d, 5e):

- (1) the registry's names are flat, so the spec's `/svc/vol/by-uuid/<hex>` and `/svc/vol/<name>` are `vol-uuid-<hex>` and `vol-<name>`
- (2) discovery runs once at start (no hot attach or removal)
- (3) the restart path (a dead `fsd` is restarted and its names republished after a 200 ms wait for the registry to mark them stale) is not exercised in QEMU: there is no way for the suite to kill one `fsd`
- (4) grants are built (`/etc/trusted`, `/mnt/<name>`; see FS-SPEC 6) but coarse: the grant list is the shell's, the launcher and `run` programs get none, the shell opens the volume once at program start (a restart of the volume server mid-run breaks that program's connection), `open_retry` is used by the shell and `init` only, a missing volume is silently skipped, and `ls`/`cat`/libc know only prefix entries (no per-program view of `/mnt`)
- (5) the QEMU test has one volume and one junk disk: the duplicate, blank-disk and attach-order cases (spec checks 10 and 10b) are covered by the pure `mounts::plan` tests only
- (6) the test formats the disk in the guest (setup), the host `fsck-doofs` checks it afterwards
- (7) a failed publish loses the volume server (it is restarted, up to 5 times).

## Why it was acceptable

Enough to serve and restart volumes on QEMU.

## Proper fix, and when

- (4) per-program manifests with S10; (3) with a kill hook in the suite
- (5) when the harness can attach more disks without renumbering.
