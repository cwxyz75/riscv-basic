## Shortcut

(1) `Sched2::settle` calls `pull`, which locks every other hart briefly (and both harts when it moves something) at every tick and every reschedule: O(harts) uncontended lock round trips per tick. The published hints do not include the best queued priority, so the pull cannot skip a hart without looking. (2) A run-queue lock released after a change recomputes the 63-entry `would_run` mask (63 class calls), also on every tick. (3) `balance` and `push_queue` look at hints and queues the same way.

## Why it was acceptable

Correctness first (OD125); the suite passes at one to four harts and the cost is a few hundred instructions per tick. `bkl_profile` and `os-bench` are the measure.

## Proper fix, and when

Publish `queued_top` and a changed-bit, let `pull` skip harts whose hint says nothing outranks this hart (keeping the push the old code did for the thread it found), and recompute the mask only when the running priority or the idle state changed. With L12, when ticks stop taking the big lock and the profile shows this cost.
