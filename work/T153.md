## Shortcut

Each gate job (`smoke a`, `smoke b`, `release smoke`, `shell test`, the soak jobs) runs `apt-get install qemu-system-riscv opensbi clang llvm`. On 2026-11-18 that step took between 20 seconds and 6 minutes (PR 238's `shell test`, `smoke b` and `release smoke` all hit the 7-minute cap with the step at 5.5 minutes and were cancelled; PR 235's `home computer` job had the same). Re-running passes.

## Why it was acceptable

A re-run fixes it; the gate's budget (OD121) was set against the boot, not the install.

## Risk

Spurious red gates cost a re-run (about 5 minutes each) and train the habit of ignoring red.

## Proper fix, and when

Cache what the step installs: an `actions/cache` of the downloaded `.deb`s (`/var/cache/apt/archives`, keyed on the runner image and the package list) restored before `apt-get install`, or a pinned container image with QEMU preinstalled. Soon, as it hits every PR.

## Note (2026-11-18)

A composite action `.github/actions/install-qemu` now restores a cache of the downloaded `.deb`s (`~/apt-archives`) and installs with `--no-download`; a cold or stale cache falls back to `apt-get update` and a normal install. Whether it helps is for CI to show (the first run on a branch is cold). Close this item when two or three PRs in a row have install steps under a minute.
