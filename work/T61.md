## Shortcut

**A binding outlives the handle it was made through (design review D17).** Closing, narrowing or sending the handle that was used to `port_bind` does not end the binding: the port keeps watching the object. Authority is not extended (the port's owner already held WAIT when it bound), but a revoked right is not honoured afterwards.

## Why it was acceptable

Bindings are made by trusted servers today.

## Proper fix, and when

Record the binder's process and handle on the binding and end it on close, replace or transfer of that handle (the hook is the forced touch the handle table already makes). Plan: resolved by SCHED-SMP-SPEC task 21.
