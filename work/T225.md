L20's accessor step (2026-11-23): the user state is reached only through `user::with_state(|s| ...)` (the object lock, a closure, no reference that outlives it); 97 sections were converted by script and the rest by hand. What is left of "no function returns a `&'static mut` to shared state" (KERNEL-V1-SPEC 6):
- ~~`user::state_locked()`~~ (done 2026-11-24: the accessor is gone; `dispatch` is a `with_state` around `dispatch_in(s, ..)`, the loader's three sections and `take_interrupt_held` and the register-token section are closures, the fault path clones the address space's `Arc` out of the section; k shard at one and four harts passes).
- `arch/riscv64/fp.rs`'s `state()` (the floating-point tables, under the big lock at the trap's first act): its own lock or per-hart ownership (T166 has the claim protocol).
- `kmem::Kmem`, `Mem`, `Frames`, `JobsRef`: structs of `&'static mut` fields next to the guards they come from (`inside`), the reference never outliving the struct; the type-level fix is closures (`with_mem(|m| ...)`) as for the user state.
- the `static mut` list of KT14's audit.
Why acceptable: every use is behind a lock that the accessor asserts or the guard holds; nothing relies on a human reading it, and KT14 audits each. Proper fix: with KT14.
