## Shortcut

**Hand-tuned caps** (OD50) that were sized to a fixed 8 MiB heap. Since slice T step 2 the heap grows (4 MiB headroom at safe points, from the frame database, never shrinking), so the caps are no longer the only thing between a program and an out-of-memory panic, but they are still the constants chosen for 8 MiB and a single syscall must still not allocate more than the headroom.

## Why it was acceptable

Works; audited.

## Proper fix, and when

Size caps from the heap or let the heap grow from the frame allocator; revisit with T1.
