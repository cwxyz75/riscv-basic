## Note (2026-11-18, plan 4: the v1 kernel)

Amended by KERNEL-V1-SPEC 2.4 and 5.1 (OD127, OD131). **Sstc is required: there is no SBI `set_timer` fallback and no probe**; `kernel/src/timer.rs`'s SBI path is deleted. **There is no periodic tick at all**: task 28 (tickless idle and tickless single-runner, L28) is built in this item, not later; the starvation watchdog becomes a timer entry in debug builds. The timer frequency comes from one `timebase()` function (a constant until PL4). Acceptance as the roadmap entry says, plus: an idle hart takes zero timer interrupts over a second; a hart with one runnable thread and no deadline takes none; the per-hart interrupt counters prove both; `sleep_never_wakes_early` (AB2) still passes with slack. Plan 4 (`WORK-PLAN.md`) orders this item and carries the owner's go-ahead for it; the sentence above about waiting for a go-ahead and the ordering gate no longer applies (the order is the plan's, changed only on the evidence of the lock profile, recorded as a decision).
