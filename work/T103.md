## Shortcut

**Secondary harts that fail to start, or are found already running, are logged and skipped** (S5; split out of T37, whose other items are resolved, R27): the boot goes on with fewer harts rather than failing.

## Why it was acceptable

A machine that cannot start a hart is not one we target.

## Proper fix, and when

Treat it as a boot failure under the release build, or retry.
