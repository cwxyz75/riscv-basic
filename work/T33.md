## Shortcut

**Programs cannot write files**: `sh` has no redirection and no write program, and the shell's root connection is read-only.

## Why it was acceptable

S4's acceptance only reads; `/tmp` is reachable only through test commands.

## Proper fix, and when

A `write`/redirection slice with a per-program writable-`/tmp` powerbox entry.
