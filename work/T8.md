## Shortcut

**Handle tables are sized for the worst case** (4096 entries per process, about 4.6 MiB over 64 processes), allocated up front.

## Why it was acceptable

Fits the 8 MiB heap today.

## Proper fix, and when

Grow on demand with a hard cap; fits better once quotas exist (T1).
