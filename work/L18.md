## Note (2026-11-18, plan 4: the v1 kernel)

Amended by KERNEL-V1-SPEC 5.4: the ASID allocator has **generations** (a global generation in the high bits of a per-address-space tag; when the hart's ASID space is used up the generation advances and each hart flushes everything at its next address-space switch), so it does not depend on a small process cap, and there is **no flush-on-every-switch mode**: a hart with a short ASID field just advances generations more often. `ran_on` gains the rule that a hart's bit is cleared when it goes offline (PM1 uses it). TLB1 is restated per generation; `fault-asid-reuse-no-flush` stays the negative control, plus a host model of the allocator across a generation change. Plan 4 (`WORK-PLAN.md`) orders this item and carries the owner's go-ahead for it; the sentence above about waiting for a go-ahead and the ordering gate no longer applies (the order is the plan's, changed only on the evidence of the lock profile, recorded as a decision).
