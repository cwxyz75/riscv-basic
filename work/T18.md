## Shortcut

**Shell has no redirection, variables, globbing, job control**; one foreground pipeline; confined `CWD` is read-only.

## Why it was acceptable

Out of S4's acceptance.

## Proper fix, and when

A later slice: redirection through a pump in the shell; creation rights on a writable cwd.

(Migrated from the old TIDY-UP register, section "Services and userland", 2026-11-18.)

Mostly superseded: the shell is `ish` (OD110), with redirection, bindings, globbing, background jobs, `wait` and `kill`. Left:
