## Note (2026-11-18, plan 4: the v1 kernel)

Amended by KERNEL-V1-SPEC section 6: when `LEGACY` becomes `OBJ`, the accessors that return `&'static mut` to shared state (`kmem::get`, `user::state`, `thread::kernel`) are **deleted in this item**; every shared structure is reachable only through its lock's guard. Acceptance gains: a grep check in the docs check that no `static mut` holding shared state and no function returning `&'static mut` remains in `kernel/src` outside per-hart records and boot-only code. Plan 4 (`WORK-PLAN.md`) orders this item and carries the owner's go-ahead for it; the sentence above about waiting for a go-ahead and the ordering gate no longer applies (the order is the plan's, changed only on the evidence of the lock profile, recorded as a decision).

## Progress (2026-11-23)

**First step built:** a syscall from user mode no longer runs under the big lock from trap entry to exit; the object calls are one section each, every other call takes the lock per section (amendment of the same date). One k-shard boot at one hart and one at four passed (282 tests); the rest is the feature gate and the epic gate. **Second step written, not booted:** the commit to the waiters moves to the end of every outermost `irq::without` (`commit_if_dirty`, `Registry::has_pending`: W1' by construction), and the trap tail takes the lock only when a switch is owed, the heap is low, or the return is not quiet (`return_to_user_is_quiet`: the attention epoch and `activate_current_quiet`). **Next:** (1) check both steps at the epic gate and bisect there if it is red, (2) `LEGACY` renamed `OBJ` with the order `OBJ` before `AS` and the non-object users of the big lock (heap service, kstack, console bridges, timers) moved off it, (3) the accessors (`state()`, `kmem::get()`, `kernel()`) behind guards, (4) T215's chunked copies out of the object section, (5) the object section itself split per call (3.13) when a profile says so.

**Third step written (2026-11-23):** `LEGACY` is `OBJ` (class 1, `klock`, `bkl.rs`, the tests; the numbers closed up), and `thread.rs`'s scheduler and thread-table sections no longer take it (`irq::off`, 35 sites; the four that evaluate user-state conditions stay `irq::without`). **Left of L20:** T224 (the kernel's page table, the heap and the stack slots under locks of their own, so `heap::grow`, `kstack` and `kmem::get` stop asserting `OBJ`), the accessors behind guards, T215's chunked copies, the sweep and roots of T206 out of the object section, and deleting the reentrancy.

**Fourth step written (2026-11-23):** the user state is reached only through `user::with_state(|s| ...)`; the last unchecked accessors are T225. **Left of L20:** T206's near-the-cap sweep and the check of all of it at the epic gate (T215's chunked copies, `channel_call` included, and the deletion of the reentrancy of `OBJ` are done, 2026-11-23: `with_state` assumes the lock, `locked_state` takes it, `bkl::ensure` / `any_state` serve the entry points reached both ways, and `lock` panics on a second take; the dual-context helpers are a follow-up for T239: splitting them so each has one context).
