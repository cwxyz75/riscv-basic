title: devmgr starts drivers in jobs with a real-time ceiling and a budget (SCHED-SMP-SPEC 4.9, the last line of L8's policy)
refs: [SCHED-SMP-SPEC, L8]
---

**What.** 4.9 says driver jobs made by `devmgr` get an RT ceiling and a budget, so interrupt threads can sit in the real-time band. L8 built the mechanism and the rest of the policy (the launcher's 20 and no budget, the session job's 31); `devmgr` still spawns `blkd` and the other drivers into its own job, which has the root's 63 and the whole window, and no driver runs a thread at a real-time priority.

**Why it waits.** A driver job's ceiling and budget are only meaningful once something enforces the budget (L25) and a driver has an interrupt thread that asks for the band; `devmgr` also has no job handle of its own to make child jobs from (`job_root` goes to `init` and `sessiond`), so the change is a handle hand-over in `init` plus a job per driver. Doing it now would set numbers nothing reads. The risk of waiting: none today (drivers run at the default priority); the root-sized ceiling is the status quo.

**Proper fix.** With L25: `init` gives `devmgr` a MANAGE handle to a `drivers` job below the root with `max_prio` 50 and `rt_budget` 20 ms per window; `devmgr` makes a job per driver below it; the first driver interrupt thread that wants the band (HD items) sets it, and a suite test shows a driver job cannot exceed its ceiling.
