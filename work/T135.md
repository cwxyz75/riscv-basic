## Shortcut

**A flaky shuttle test in CI** (2026-11-17): `klock`'s `spin_concurrency::the_lock_excludes` (run under the shuttle model checker in the `host` job) failed once on PR 205 (`shuttle-0.8.1/src/runtime/execution.rs:203`, a panic inside the checker's execution) and passed on rerun; `klock` was not touched by that PR. Not reproduced. Shuttle explores schedules at random, so a failure can mean a real interleaving bug in the lock or a checker limit (its step or thread bounds); the log was not kept past the failure line.

## Why it was acceptable

Cannot say which without the schedule.

## Proper fix, and when

If it recurs, keep the shuttle seed and schedule from the log (`SHUTTLE_*` replay) and bisect the lock.
