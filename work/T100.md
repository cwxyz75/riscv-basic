## Shortcut

**The socket shim (S9 step 6) leftovers**:

- (1) a socket's mutex is held for the whole of a blocking call, so a second thread using the same descriptor waits (and `poll` on a descriptor another thread is blocked in `accept` on waits too)
- (2) `poll` creates a port per call instead of reusing one per thread (S9-SPEC 9.3)
- (3) `sendmsg`/`recvmsg`, `accept` of the peer address for a listener's posted pair beyond what `Accepted` carries, `getaddrinfo` and `/etc` files are not provided (DNS is step 7)
- (4) `SO_RCVBUF`/`SO_SNDBUF` apply at the next `connect` or `listen` only, as specified
- (5) a blocking `connect` waits at most the send timeout (none by default) and the stack's own 15 s limit ends it
- (6) UDP loopback (own address and 127.x) was added to `netcore` for the shim's sake and is not in NET-SPEC; there is no ICMP error for a closed local port, only a counter
- (7) `getrandom` serves at most 256 bytes a call and needs `random` in the program's `/svc` view; the view the launcher gives programs does not include it yet
- (8) the C socket test ran once with a flaky failure at `-smp 1` (a reset check) that did not repeat in three more runs; its checks now say which step failed.

## Why it was acceptable

The shim's decisions are host-tested (`sockshim`) and the C test `ctest sockets` runs on the OS (about 40 checks).

## Risk

(1) a multithreaded server that shares a listener will serialise on it.

## Proper fix, and when

Step 8 and the launcher work for (7).
