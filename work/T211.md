title: GP1 is excused on a hart whose real-time share is spent (L25): find why a normal thread then waits on another hart
refs: [SCHED-SMP-SPEC, L25]
---

**What.** L25's throttle (NM1) holds a hart's real-time queue back when its share of the window is spent and the normal band has work. The GP1 audit (`kcore/src/sched_smp.rs` `audit`) and the two walk references (`kcore/tests/sched2_smp_model.rs`, `sched2_kernel_walk.rs`) skip such a hart for every waiting thread, not only real-time ones.

**Why it is acceptable for now.** The only violations seen are a normal thread waiting on another hart a little longer than strict priority allows while a hart is in the throttle (never a lost thread, a double dispatch or a real-time thread running past its share: the NM1 and demotion tests check those directly). The Fable review of PR 390 asked for the narrow form (excuse only real-time candidates) and it fails: `gp1_and_gp2_hold_on_random_walks_over_one_to_eight_harts` seed 904 step 331 (hart 3 runs priority 15, thread 66 of priority 19 waits on hart 4, whose queue is led by held-back real-time threads), and seed 964 step 67 without the mid-switch excuse.

**Proper fix.** Find which settle or pull misses it (the pull of the best normal thread behind a held-back real-time queue, or the push offer that should mark hart 3), fix it, and narrow the excuse to real-time candidates. Do it with the next change to placement (SC2) or when the walks are next touched.
