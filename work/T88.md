## Shortcut

**S8 review findings not fixed** (Fable, `docs/records/reviews/2026-11-15-s8-network.md`; findings 1 to 4, 8, 9, 10 and parts of 12, 13 are fixed):

- (5) `netd` gets a registry connection with all rights, not PUBLISH restricted to its three names
- (6) a released device claim revokes nothing (a former claimant keeps its MMIO, DMA and interrupt handles; the supervisor's children should live in a job it kills; a repeated claim-and-drop pins 130 DMA pages a round); the driver's pool handles now carry only DMA and TRANSFER (it cannot map them)
- (7) every forwarded open to `netd` is its own 32-connection account, so a few service connections can use up the 256-connection pool: one parent account per class with its own limit is needed
- (11) a driver-only restart can race a stack that is mid-pass (a stale index store can cost another violation restart): start the new driver after the stack acknowledges the new control channel
- (12) the rest of `netsup`'s control-channel and early-return handle leaks
- (13) `tx_ready` has no minimum length check in the driver
- (14) any `oflags` bit grants both privileges, the request header's flags are ignored, a wrong-kind send is a notice, `Echo::ping` fails on a `SendFailed` notice for any cookie
- (15) the pcap check is checksums only (no counts of ARP, DISCOVER, echo), `close()` ignores its job leak check, NET1 in QEMU, NET3 as a rights check, the driver-side bad-slot control (`fault-bad-slot-tx`), Q13's renewal and the 'no new DISCOVER' checks of Q12 and Q13 are not written
