## Shortcut

**Many hard limits assume QEMU as the target machine** (owner, 2026-11-14): thread, object, handle, region, IPC-byte, binding and stack-size constants (`abi/src/lib.rs`, OD50, T13), the 256 MiB RAM assumption, the 16 KiB kernel stack, the 1 ms and 10 ms scheduler constants. Until reviewed, new limits in this plan are expressed per MiB of RAM or as job-quota defaults, not fresh constants (the thread backstop is 16 per MiB of RAM, 4096 at 256 MiB)

## Why it was acceptable

Correct on the one machine that exists; raising them is a one-line change each

## Proper fix, and when

A review of every hard limit: classify each as a safety backstop, a policy default or a mechanism constant; derive backstops from RAM and hart count at boot; make policy defaults configurable (boot parameters, manifest) with a tight profile for small embedded machines and a large one for real computers; require that everything scales, with a test that boots at two RAM sizes. Do it before the first non-QEMU target, or when slice T finishes the growable heap, whichever is first
