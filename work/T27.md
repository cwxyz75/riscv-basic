## Shortcut

**Wire formats beyond the startup message** (console control, `ReadDir` entry packing, file framing) were defined in `proto` and its goldens, not first in the spec; the spec now points at those goldens as normative.

## Why it was acceptable

The spec stayed at the level of operations.

## Proper fix, and when

Spec appendix with the byte layouts when memfs and consoled land (S4c/S4d).
