## Shortcut

**Ctrl-D typed while a job runs is delivered to the shell later** (the job does not read it) and then ends the shell; `cat` on the terminal consumes it as data. Terminal type-ahead of 0x03/0x04 during a job is not discarded.

## Why it was acceptable

Matches the documented "no end of stream on the terminal"; harmless.

## Proper fix, and when

Discard lone 0x04 delivered while a foreground job is registered, once the console knows whether the job reads the terminal.
