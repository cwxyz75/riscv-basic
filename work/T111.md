## Shortcut

**doofs format leftovers** (filesystem step 2):

- (1) the superblock carries no counters (entry count, used blocks): `fsck` computes them by the walk
- (2) mount walks the whole tree to rebuild the free map (time grows with the volume; fine for the sizes QEMU uses, an incremental or persisted map is a later optimisation)
- (3) the free list is a `Vec<u64>` of every free block (8 bytes per free block: 2 MiB per GiB of 4 KiB blocks)
- (4) a device resized after `mkfs` is refused (`SizeMismatch`), no grow
- (5) the tools are `mkfs-doofs` etc., not `mkfs.doofs` (Cargo)
- (6) a volume has no format-version migration, only version 1
- (7) `check` reads every slot's tree in full (a slow fsck on a big image).

## Why it was acceptable

Correct and verified by the tests; none is reachable by a program yet.

## Proper fix, and when

- (2) and
- (3) with fsd if volumes grow past a few GiB
- (4) with `volmgr`
- (6) when the format changes.
