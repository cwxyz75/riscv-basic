**What.** The first soak since phase 8 (run 45164224295, `main` at the phase 10 merge): `normal`, `release` and `lossy relay` passed. Found:
1. `round-robin` and the round-robin leg of `smp` failed `a_call_lends_its_priority_to_the_server` and `pi_futex_bounds_inversion`: both are controls that need a starved low-priority thread, which round-robin does not starve. They were never in `PRIORITY_ONLY` (added with L22, PR 417; no soak ran since). Fixed (this change).
2. `negative controls` : `unmap_while_siblings_touch` was reported as passing on the kernel with no shootdown (then the run hung to the 240 s timeout): the control's race did not show on the hosted runner. Not diagnosed.
3. `miri` and `AIA, RVA23, no disk, serial` were cancelled at the 40 minute job limit; `smp` took 18 minutes for the suite at four harts (`a_thread_hog_cannot_exhaust_the_kernel` 35 to 68 s).
**Why acceptable.** Soak only; nothing here gates a merge.
**Risk.** The shootdown control may be vacuous (a regression in the shootdown would not be caught); the time-outs hide whatever those two jobs would show.
**Proper fix.** For 2, rerun the control in a loop (`make os-repeat`) to see how often it is vacuous, and strengthen the test (more touches, retry) if so. For 3, raise `timeout-minutes` on those jobs or split them, then rerun when the owner asks.
