title: SC1 leftovers: per-hart records and boot and emergency stacks are static for 64 harts, not allocated at boot
refs: [SC1, KERNEL-V1-SPEC]
---

KERNEL-V1-SPEC 2.3 says per-hart records, boot stacks and emergency stacks are allocated at boot for the harts found. SC1 made `MAX_HARTS` 64 and took the harts from `/cpus`, but `percpu::HARTS`, `percpu::EMERGENCY` and `smp::BOOT_STACKS` are still static arrays (about 2.6 MiB of `.bss` at 64: 64 x 32 KiB boot stacks, 64 x 8 KiB emergency stacks, the records), as are the arrays in `chaos`, `latency` and `fp` (`[T; MAX_HARTS]`). Why acceptable: the memory is cheap and zero-filled on the one target, the boot hart's record has to exist before the heap does, and nothing else depends on it. Risk: a machine with few harts wastes the memory; a machine with more than 64 harts is capped at 64 with a boot line. Proper fix: keep the boot hart's record static, allocate the others (and the stacks, from frames, through the direct map) once the harts are counted, and make the three debug arrays vectors; then the `.bss` falls back to the 8-hart size. The acceptance boots (`-smp 8`, 16 and 32 on the `k` shard) and the 16 and 32 hart scaling checks of SCHED-SMP-SPEC 6.1 are soak runs the owner asks for.
