## Shortcut

**Syscalls are non-preemptible and now also serialise every hart** through the big kernel lock: a long syscall (64 KiB copies, object sweeps that are O(live objects), `reap_vmos`) stalls every other hart's kernel entries, and the lock is held across the whole of each. Latency of a high-priority thread's kernel entry now depends on the longest region on any hart.

## Why it was acceptable

Short in practice; user code runs in parallel.

## Proper fix, and when

Per-object locks on the hot paths (scheduler, channels, handle tables) with the lock order extended (rung 3 of OS-ARCHITECTURE section 7), or bound the work per call; measure with the S2 latency test under `-smp 4`. Plan: resolved step by step by SCHED-SMP-SPEC tasks 12 to 20.
